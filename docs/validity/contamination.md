# Public-bank contamination

Once released, the public synthetic bank is inspectable and contamination is
unavoidable. Public scores therefore are not secret-test claims. Reproduction
must pin the Bench commit, bank digest, evaluator commit, judge configuration
digest, receipt digests, and Coffee Chat CalVer release.

Prospective release evidence records the bank before candidate evaluation. A
held bridge partition may test transfer to separately governed material, but
it remains distinct from the public bank and does not retroactively make a
public score secret. Reports must state exposure limitations and keep
implementation-test results, evaluation reports, and benchmark-validity
evidence separate.

Judge receipts retain only campaign/configuration/tariff digests, atom and
attempt identity, requested/resolved model, prompt/response digests, supplied
token usage, integer nano-USD reservation/settlement, remaining budget, and
explicit terminal or budget-stop state. They must not retain raw prompts, raw
provider responses, transport headers, or credentials. This supports audit of
the bounded public method without turning the public bank into a secret test.
