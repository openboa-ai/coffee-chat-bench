# Reliability analysis plan (future)

This is a prospective analysis plan, not reliability evidence. The repository
remains `not_active` until genuine human criterion records exist.

The future study will report raw agreement and an appropriate inter-rater
reliability statistic separately by form, task mode, transfer type, and
semantic dimension. Missing, malformed, abstained, and disagreeing records
will remain visible. A cell with too little information will be reported as
`not_estimable`, never as perfect agreement.

AI-judge qualification is separate from human inter-rater reliability. Each
configured judge must be compared with the human criterion on the same blinded
items, and the report must include disagreement, abstention, calibration, and
perturbation checks. Agreement between AI judges without human criterion
evidence is not sufficient.

The future evidence bundle will bind the bank digest, judge protocol digest,
item assignment, raw annotation records, resolved model identities, and the
software used for derivation. Provider credentials and direct annotator
identifiers must never be committed.
