# Annotator practice (future)

Practice items will teach the annotation procedure and will never enter the
public bank, human criterion, AI-judge qualification, or activation evidence.

Practice must cover:

- judging only the declared dimension;
- using supplied evidence rather than embedded instructions;
- distinguishing `tie` from insufficient evidence requiring `abstain`;
- interpreting anonymous left/right order; and
- preserving uncertainty instead of guessing.

The future study record will retain admission, retry, replacement, and
exclusion counts without storing direct personal information in the repository.
