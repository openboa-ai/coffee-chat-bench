# Task 4C2 report — committed campaign parity and full-bank calibration

## Scope

Task 4C2 turns the authored public PCDA sources and their generated campaign
into a reproducible repository artifact. It does not add a Coffee Chat Product
candidate, a live Harbor host run, a provider call, a performance report, or an
activation decision.

## Implemented contract

- `bank/blueprints/{product,research,operations}.json` is the sealed authored
  admission source. `bank/campaign/**` is a generated-only public publication
  source, with the existing marker retained.
- The parity test parses all three blueprints and the perspective catalog,
  rematerializes a temporary campaign, then compares every generated byte with
  the committed campaign. That comparison covers catalog/source digests, the
  prospective digest, selected-bank digest, all campaign counts, the 144-record
  ledger, and all 96 selected cases.
- `calibrate-bank <bank-root> <empty-workspace>` validates the complete bank,
  projects every admitted case under T0, T1-A, and T1-B exactly once, produces
  Oracle/no-op/list-all artifacts, and removes its temporary workspace by
  default. A non-empty or symlinked workspace is refused before it is used.
- The calibration invokes the verifier copied by `projectHarborTask` through
  one bounded Python batch process. It does not duplicate the verifier scoring
  logic. Every report record keeps case, family, condition, trial, and source
  identities.
- A valid control tuple is conjunctive: Oracle becomes `measured` with
  `accepted: true` and no critical failure; no-op is `candidate_invalid`; and
  list-all is `candidate_failure`. A projection, process, verifier, or malformed
  batch-output problem becomes a named invalid calibration failure instead of
  candidate credit or an omitted denominator.
- `reportDigest` is derived from stable release, bank, count, projection, and
  failure data only. It excludes workspace paths and elapsed time.

## Boundary

This is deterministic synthetic control calibration of the public bank and its
projected verifier. It proves neither a candidate's value nor Coffee Chat
performance, does not make a validity conclusion, and leaves activation at
`not_active`.

## Projection-failure retention fix — 2026-08-12

An independent review found that a `projectHarborTask` or control-artifact
generation exception escaped the projection loop and reached the bank-level
catch. That replaced every known projection identity with one `bank` failure
and discarded projections that had already completed.

The calibration loop now records each failed projection as an explicit
`projection` calibration failure with its exact case, family, condition, trial,
and source-digest identities, then continues with the remaining projections.
The failed trial identity comes from the projector's canonical trial-ID
derivation. Successfully projected entries still reach the bounded verifier
batch; a failed projection is not converted to a candidate result. The fixed
denominators remain 288 projections and 864 controls, while the reported
projected/completed counts remain the observed partial totals.
